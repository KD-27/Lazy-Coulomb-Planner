# Controlled benchmark, planner v2.1.0

Planner-only comparison of the Lazy Coulomb Planner (LCP) with NavFn, Smac 2D
and Theta\* on generated maps, varying one factor at a time around a base map
(50 m square, 10 % occupied, random circles). Run on 15 November 2026, 16:04 to
16:37, on the tagged planner `v2.1.0`, compiled optimised (Release, `-O3`).

This is the seed-1 suite. `../controlled_seeds/` repeats the density, size and
U-shape maps with four more seeds and gives the results with their spread; use
that for the headline numbers.

## Setup

- 21 maps: obstacle density 0 to 45 % (11 maps), map size 10 to 200 m (5,
  sharing the base), obstacle shape (circle, square, concave U), corridor width
  between 4 m blocks (0.8 to 3 m). Generated by `benchmark/generate_map.py`
  with seed 1; `maps/manifest.csv` has the arguments and a checksum of each. The
  maps shared with the v2.0.0 suite are byte-identical to it.
- 100 random start/goal pairs per map (fixed seed, at least 3 m apart, on cells
  with cost below 210). Every pair is planned 5 times by every planner; the time
  used is the median of the five, as reported by `planner_server`.
- Costmap: static map plus inflation, robot radius 0.22 m, inflation radius
  0.7 m, cost scaling 3.0. All planners at their Nav2 defaults. `LCP_lazy` is
  LCP with the grid fallback off. Path reuse is off for both LCP entries.
- A pair "has a route" by ground truth computed from the map
  (`benchmark/routability.py`). A path counts as solved only if it never touches
  a cell with cost 253 or more.
- Machine: Intel Core i7-13620H, 15 GB RAM, Ubuntu 24.04, ROS 2 Jazzy,
  Nav2 1.3.13.

Reproduce: `benchmark/run_suite.py benchmark/suites/controlled.yaml <out>`, then
`benchmark/routability.py <out>` and `benchmark/report_suite.py <out>`.

## Files

`tables.md` (all tables), `plots.png`, `summary.csv` and `summary_truth.csv`
(one row per map and planner), `<map>.csv` (every planner on every pair),
`suite.yaml` and `planners.yaml` (the exact configuration), `maps/`.

## Findings (single seed; spreads are in `../controlled_seeds/`)

| | LCP median | Fastest grid planner | LCP faster by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 m map, 10 % | 0.10 ms | 0.79 ms | 8× |
| 25 m | 0.21 ms | 3.90 ms | 19× |
| 50 m (base) | 0.44 ms | 13.28 ms | 30× |
| 100 m | 0.75 ms | 42.36 ms | 56× |
| 200 m | 3.03 ms | 135.03 ms | 45× |
| Square obstacles | 0.35 ms | 10.62 ms | 31× |
| Concave U obstacles | 2.34 ms | 13.18 ms | 5.6× |
| Corridors 0.8 m | 1.58 ms | 4.21 ms | 2.7× |
| Corridors 1.2 m | 0.68 ms | 7.31 ms | 11× |
| Corridors 2.0 m | 0.53 ms | 7.44 ms | 14× |
| Corridors 3.0 m | 0.34 ms | 9.18 ms | 27× |

- LCP solves every routable pair on all 21 maps with a clean path.
- Its slowest plan on any of these maps is 13 ms (on the 200 m map); the 95th
  percentile is at most 9 ms.
- On the 200 m map Smac 2D fails 42 of 100 pairs ("exceeded maximum
  iterations", its default limit), which also makes its median look fast.
- Paths are 1 to 3 % shorter than NavFn's and run closer to obstacles: average
  costmap cost along the path 16 on the base map against 0.4 for NavFn.
- Paths with a corner sharper than 90°: none or one on most maps, 6 of 100 on
  the 100 m map and 10 of 100 on the 200 m map.

## Against v2.0.0

The same seed-1 maps were measured on `v2.0.0` in
`../../2026-11-15/controlled_v2.0.0/`, but that build was not optimised, so its
times are about 2.5 times too high and the two cannot be compared directly. On
the base map v2.0.0 took 1.41 ms there; rebuilt optimised it takes about 0.5 ms
(`../../2026-11-15/dev_v2.1/`), and v2.1.0 takes 0.44 ms. The larger gains are on
the hard maps: concave obstacles 12.5 ms (unoptimised v2.0.0) to 2.3 ms, and the
slowest plan on the 200 m map 531 ms to 13 ms.

## Limits

One seed per configuration here. All planners at default settings; one costmap
configuration; one machine.
